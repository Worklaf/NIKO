Data Deletion

You have the right to request complete deletion of your personal data from N1K∅ Music at any time.

What Will Be Deleted

  • Account and authentication data (Google, Email, PIN)
  • Listening history, likes, and dislikes
  • Comments and feedback submissions
  • Custom playlists and saved preferences
  • Push notification subscriptions
  • Offline cached tracks and local storage data

How to Delete Your Data

1

Fastest Way — Feedback Form

The quickest way to request deletion is through the 💬 Feedback button on the main N1K∅ Music page. Select "Other" as the type and write "Delete my account and all data". Include the email you used to sign in. We process these requests within 48 hours.

2

Email Request

Send an email with the subject "Data Deletion Request" to niko.music.app@gmail.com. Include your registered email address. We will confirm deletion within 48 hours and complete it within 7 business days.

3

Revoke Third-Party Access

You can also disconnect the Service from your Google account directly:

🔴 Google

Go to myaccount.google.com/permissions, find N1K∅ Music, and click "Remove access". To delete data from our servers, submit a request via Step 1 or 2.

4

Clear Local Data (Optional)

To remove cached files and cookies from your device:

Desktop: Browser Settings → Privacy → Clear browsing data → Select "Cookies and site data" for niko-vert.vercel.app

Mobile (PWA): Uninstall the app or clear app data in system settings.

📧 Prefer email? Send your request to:

niko.music.app@gmail.com

We will respond and confirm deletion within 48 hours.

Data Retention

After receiving your deletion request, we will:

  • Acknowledge receipt within 24 hours.
  • Permanently delete your data from our Firebase database within 7 business days.
  • Delete associated audio/cover files from Cloudflare R2 storage (if you uploaded content).
  • Send you a final confirmation email once deletion is complete.

Note: Some anonymized analytics data (e.g., total play counts) may be retained in aggregated form that cannot be linked back to you.
