Data Deletion

You have the right to request complete deletion of your personal data from N1K∅ Music.

What Will Be Deleted

  • Account and authentication data (Google, Email, Facebook)
  • Listening history and likes
  • Comments and feedback submissions
  • Local playlists and settings
  • Push subscriptions and notifications

How to Delete Your Data

1

Automatic Deletion (coming soon)

An "Delete Account" button will be available in your profile settings. Until then, please use the manual method below.

2

Manual Request (recommended)

Send an email with the subject "Data Deletion Request" to the address below. Include the email you used to sign in. We will delete all data within 7 business days.

3

Revoke Facebook Access

If you signed in via Facebook, you can also revoke access in your Facebook app settings:
facebook.com/settings → Apps and Websites

4

Clear Local Data

To remove cached data and cookies from your device: Browser Settings → Privacy → Clear Site Data → niko-vert.vercel.app

📧 Send your deletion request to:

privacy@niko-vert.vercel.app

We will respond and confirm deletion within 48 hours.
