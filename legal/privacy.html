Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 29, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum data necessary to operate the service:

  • Account data: email, username, avatar (when signing in via Google, Email, or Facebook).
  • Music preferences: likes, dislikes, listening history, playlists.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser User-Agent, language settings — for abuse prevention.
  • Cookies: authentication tokens, language and player settings.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To authenticate you and personalize the interface.
  • To display listening history and recommendations.
  • For analytics (Firebase Analytics) — anonymized data only.
  • To send push notifications about new tracks (only with your consent).

3. Facebook (Meta) Login

When you log in via Facebook, we receive only:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Profile/avatar link

We do not post on your behalf, do not read your friends list, and do not share your data with third parties.

4. Data Storage & Security

Data is stored in Firebase (Google Cloud). All connections are protected with SSL/TLS. Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text.

5. Your Rights

  • Right to access your personal data.
  • Right to correct inaccurate information.
  • Right to delete your account and all associated data.
  • Right to withdraw consent for data processing.
Delete your data: You can request complete deletion at any time on the Data Deletion page or by emailing us below.

6. Contact

For privacy-related questions:
