Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 29, 2026

1. Who We Are

N1K∅ Music is a private music streaming platform. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal data when you use our Service.

2. What Data We Collect

We collect only the minimum data necessary to provide the service:

  • Authentication data: email, display name, profile photo — provided by your chosen sign-in method (see Section 3).
  • Music activity: likes, dislikes, listening history, playlists you create, comments.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type, device language, screen size — for security and abuse prevention.
  • Cookies & local storage: authentication tokens, language preference, player settings, offline cache.

3. Authentication Providers

We do not store passwords. Authentication is handled by trusted third-party providers:

Google Sign-In

When you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account email, name, and profile picture. We use Google Firebase Authentication to process this securely. Google Privacy Policy

Email & Password

Email and password are handled by Firebase Authentication. Passwords are hashed by Firebase; we never see your plain-text password.

PIN Code Access (Admin / Partner)

For admin and partner accounts, we use a SHA-256 hashed PIN code stored in our database. The PIN itself is never stored in plain text.

4. How We Use Your Data

  • To authenticate you and keep you logged in.
  • To personalize your experience (language, theme, player settings).
  • To display your listening history, likes, and playlists.
  • To enable comments and social features.
  • For anonymized analytics (Firebase Analytics) to improve the Service.
  • To send push notifications about new tracks — only if you explicitly opt in.

5. Data Storage & Security

All data is stored in Firebase (Google Cloud) with industry-standard encryption. All network connections use SSL/TLS. We regularly review access logs to prevent unauthorized access.

6. Third-Party Services

We use the following services to operate the platform:

  • Firebase (Google) — authentication, database, analytics, hosting.
  • Cloudflare R2 — storage for audio files and cover images.
  • Google — optional social login and analytics.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third party.

7. Your Rights

  • Access: You can view all data associated with your account by using the Service.
  • Correction: Update your profile information through account settings.
  • Deletion: Request complete deletion of your account and all associated data at any time. See our Data Deletion page.
  • Portability: Request an export of your data (admin feature).
  • Withdraw consent: Disable push notifications or delete your account.
Data Deletion: You can request complete deletion of your data at any time via the Data Deletion page or through the Feedback form on our website.

8. Cookies & Local Storage

We use browser local storage and cookies for:

  • Keeping you signed in (auth tokens)
  • Remembering your language preference
  • Storing player settings (volume, shuffle mode)
  • Caching tracks for offline listening

You can clear this data at any time through your browser settings.

9. Children's Privacy

The Service is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe we have collected data from a minor, please contact us immediately.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of significant changes via the Service or by email if available.

11. Contact Us

For privacy-related questions, data deletion requests, or concerns:

• Use the 💬 Feedback button on the main site
• Or email: niko.music.app@gmail.com
